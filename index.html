LOCAL-FIRST, NOT CLOUDLESS

Your dreams stay yours. By default.

The persistent journal is local. Cloud AI is opt-in and scoped — it activates only when you trigger a feature that needs it, and you see exactly what's sent.

🔒
Local-first storage
Your journal lives on your device. No account, no server-side diary, no cross-device sync in MVP. You own the file.
🤝
Explicit cloud consent
Voice transcription and deep analysis send necessary content through Dreamer's backend. You consent once. You can decline and keep the local journal working.
🛡️
No journal in telemetry
Crash and analytics events never include dream titles, entities, source text, or analysis content. Sensitive payloads auto-expire on the backend.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Honest answers, no mystery.

Is Dreamer a dream dictionary? +
No. Dream dictionaries give every visitor the same symbol lookup. Dreamer grounds every interpretation in the specific dream you recorded and the personal history you've built in your world. Same dream on different nights can have very different readings.
Do my dreams get stored on a server? +
No. Your persistent journal lives on your device. Cloud AI and voice transcription are opt-in — they send only the content required to perform the operation you triggered, and you can decline and keep the local journal working.
What does "grounded interpretation" mean? +
Every interpretive claim Dreamer makes is tied to source material from the specific school lens being applied. If evidence is weak, the school abstains. If sources conflict, you see alternatives instead of a blended "final answer."
Will Dreamer diagnose me from a dream? +
No. Dreamer is non-diagnostic by design. It offers possible interpretations, never disorders, treatment, or risk inferences from dream symbolism. Interpretation appears only in Analysis contexts, not in Home or the relationship graph.
What happens after the free trial? +
Your free Premium trials are one-time only and don't renew monthly. When they're used, deep analysis requires Premium or you continue with the local journal, My World, search, and other free features.
Can I switch between iOS and Android? +
Yes. Backups are encrypted, versioned, and cross-platform. Export through your share sheet, import on the other device, enter the password. Restore purchases restores Premium entitlement on the new device.
